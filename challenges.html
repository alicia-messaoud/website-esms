Our challenges

Every challenge brings a new perspective and a chance to think differently. From uncovering hidden clues to creating practical solutions, each stage offers a unique experience designed to put your abilities into action. Explore the challenges below and discover where your strengths shine!

1. Logic Sprint

Teams solve logic puzzles and algorithmic reasoning challenges as quickly as possible.

2. Cyber Crack

A mini cybersecurity investigation where teams have to spot phishing attempts, decode secret messages, and find a final code using the clues.

3. Tech Battle

An interactive quiz about computer science, AI, programming languages, and technology in general, where both correct answers and speed can earn points.

4. Build It

The final challenge! Teams have to develop a small functional prototype to solve a real-life problem, then present their solution to the jury.
